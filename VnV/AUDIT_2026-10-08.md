# Independent audit of the SA-modifications V&V (2026-10-08)

Method: five independent reviewers (equations; unit tests; V&V numbers and NASA data; ADflow-vs-SU2; convergence,
conditions and grids), each followed by a skeptic who re-checked every finding and looked for what was missed.
10 agents, 395 tool calls, ~25 min.  Scratch evidence (scripts, logs, page renders) under the session scratchpad audit/.

## Verdict
* The equations are right.  SA-Edwards (block and blockette/ANK/NK paths), SA-comp, the Catris/Seror/conservative
  density corrections, the DADI diagonal terms and all three Tapenade AD outputs match Edwards & Chandra, the NASA TMR
  and SU2 term by term (16 checks).  Nothing wrong, no blocker.  The modified build is safe to use with ANK/NK.
* The unit tests pass as claimed (39 pass / 15 skip under testflo MPI mode, 97 s) but the test file and README are
  uncommitted, the counts depend on the runner (option leakage between parameterized classes), and no test runs a
  solver to convergence or checks a reference value.
* Every number in results/summary.md reproduces from the raw results; all ~200 transcribed NASA values match the
  report pages; the figures plot the table data.
* The validation NARRATIVE has real problems (ranked below): the Edwards & Chandra "SA" curves are the paper's
  Edwards-modified SA (mis-attributed); one virtual origin is shared by all models although the paper matched the
  inflow per model; the fine-grid SU2 runs are not converged; fin/flare have no quantified grid uncertainty.

## Adjudications by the session lead (after the agents)
* "Kussoy survey station is x = +11 cm" (convergence skeptic, flagged MAJOR): REFUTED.  Edwards & Chandra say the
  inflow was "matched at the proper distance from the flat plate leading edge (167 cm)", i.e. upstream of the apex
  (176 cm); the V&V's x = -11 cm (165 cm) is consistent within 2 cm (~1 % in theta).
* SU2's SA diffusion form: the two skeptics disagree (README says SU2 uses the TMR rho-inside form; one skeptic says
  SU2 is non-conservative).  From the SU2 v8.5.0 source the SA diffusion coefficient is kinematic ((nu + nu_t), no rho,
  turb_diffusion.hpp:75-85) while the convective flux carries rho (turb_convection.hpp:122).  Unresolved here; it only
  affects the interpretation of the 3-7 % ADflow-SU2 BASELINE gap, not the Edwards-increment check.
* Side effects of the audit on this repo: (1) tests/reg_tests/testflo_report.out (gitignored) was overwritten by the
  test reviewer's run and now holds only test_sa_corrections; the README's full-suite "181 pass" has no surviving
  artefact until the suite is rerun (~24 min).  (2) importing su2/compare.py regenerated results/su2_comparison.{json,md}
  and figures/plate_ADflow_vs_SU2.png; json/md are byte-identical to HEAD, the PNG was already modified (legend work).

## Addendum 2026-10-09: SU2 convergence fixed
The stalled SU2 second-order runs (finding "Fine-grid SU2 runs are the least converged", and in fact all three levels:
rms[rho] -4.1 / -5.2 / -5.0) were traced to a persistent residual in the few nodes at the plate leading edge.  Limiter
freezing and a 100x tighter linear solve did nothing; the Venkatakrishnan limiter (frozen after 500 iterations) with
adaptive CFL 2 -> 50 converges every grid to rms[rho] 1e-11.  All six cases were regenerated from scratch with that
recipe (su2/make_su2_case.py, run_su2_all.sh; old results in results/su2_v1_vanalbada/).  Converged comparison of the
SA-Edwards increment, SU2 - ADflow: Cf 0.10 / 0.10 / 0.12 pt, q_w 0.22 / 0.18 / 0.17 pt, theta 0.6 / 0.7 / 0.9 pt
(coarse / medium / fine); the fine-grid outliers of the stalled runs (0.34 / 0.59 pt) were convergence artefacts, and
SU2's baseline sequence is now monotonic (Cf 0.885 / 0.901 / 0.910e-3, extrapolating ~3 % below ADflow's
grid-converged 0.954e-3).  README Sec. 1.2 and the summary table were updated accordingly.
